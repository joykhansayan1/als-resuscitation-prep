Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

ALS Resuscitation Prep (“the App”) is published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we”, “us”).

Summary

The App is offline-first. We do not collect personal data through analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, accounts, or tracking. Progress stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Onboarding choices (course date, goals, daily goal, reminder preference)
  • Practice answers, bookmarks, spaced-repetition cards, and session results
  • Purchase entitlement status managed on-device by Apple StoreKit (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Billing (Android)

This data is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate a backend for this App.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime access are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your full payment card details. Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Notifications

If you enable a study reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. Notifications are not used for marketing.

Links

The App may open external sites (e.g. resus.org.uk, Apple EULA, Google Play). Those sites have their own policies.

Children

The App is intended for adult healthcare learners. It is not directed at children under 13.
